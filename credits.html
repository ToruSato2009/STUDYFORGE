Founder · Creator · Builder of STUDYFORGE

I’m a student, creator, and technology enthusiast who enjoys turning ideas into practical projects. STUDYFORGE began with a simple thought: studying can feel more manageable when goals are organised, progress is visible, and consistency is rewarded. This is an evolving project built around that idea.

Founder portrait · STUDYFORGE

My story

I’m interested in technology, design, problem-solving, and building things that can grow beyond an idea. STUDYFORGE is one step in that journey: a place to experiment, learn from real use, and make a useful tool for students.

Why STUDYFORGE?

Planning should help students get started—not overwhelm them. STUDYFORGE combines tasks, subjects, schedules, progress tracking, and gamified rewards to make daily study goals easier to see and manage.

What comes next

The current version is a starting point. Future possibilities include secure accounts, cloud synchronisation, richer analytics, and more reliable planning, verified curriculum coverage, and optional cloud sync when it is ready.

The mission

To make studying more organised, engaging, and rewarding—helping students turn their goals into consistent progress, one task at a time.
