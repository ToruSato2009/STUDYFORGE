STUDYFORGE V2 — PRIORITY & REDISTRIBUTION UPDATE

Implemented in this build:
1. Dynamic daily task redistribution
   - Adding a task keeps the newly entered duration and redistributes the remaining available time across other unfinished tasks.
   - Editing a task duration keeps the requested duration and redistributes the remaining available time.
   - Deleting a task redistributes its available time across the remaining unfinished tasks.
   - Marking a task complete preserves its duration and reallocates the remaining budget across unfinished tasks.
   - Reopening a completed task returns it to the unfinished allocation pool.
   - Completed task durations are not changed by redistribution.
   - Past dates remain read-only.
   - Each unfinished task receives at least one minute; if the budget cannot support that, the app warns instead of silently exceeding the budget.

2. Flexible Subject Priority
   - A priority notification appears in the Subjects section.
   - Students can rank every selected subject from highest to lowest using up/down controls.
   - The saved ranking remains until the student changes it.
   - New subjects are appended to the end of an already configured priority list.
   - Priority affects generated task allocations and future rebalancing.
   - Higher-ranked subjects receive a larger allocation share, while lower-ranked subjects still retain time.

3. Compatibility
   - Existing localStorage state is retained; priorityOrder is added with a safe default for older accounts.
   - This remains a local-first build. Supabase is not connected in this package.
